About Us

Our team has successfully developed a deep understanding of the Saudi market, enabling us to supply reliable, high-quality products that serve diverse industrial sectors.


Founded in 2024, Forsa General Trading was established with a clear mission: to create opportunities and deliver excellence while supporting and empowering local businesses across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

We offer a comprehensive portfolio of products and solutions tailored to meet the evolving needs of multiple industries. Our range includes construction materials, steel products, pipes, valves, tanks, cables, and other essential industrial supplies.

At Forsa, we are committed to being your trusted source for dependable, high-quality products. Our approach is built on efficiency, seamless service, and a strong customer focus — ensuring long-term partnerships based on trust and reliability.




Our Values


At Forsa General Trading, we are guided by a core set of values that shape every aspect of our business. The name Forsa, meaning ‘Opportunity’ in Arabic, reflects our commitment to seize every opportunity to support our clients and contribute to the realization of Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030. We operate with integrity, ensuring transparency and trust in all our interactions. Our dedication to excellence drives us to deliver high-quality, reliable products and services. We prioritize sustainability, ensuring that our operations contribute to the long-term success of both our clients and the broader Saudi economy. Above all, we believe in collaboration, working closely with our stakeholders to drive growth and create enduring value.

Vision


Inspired by Saudi Arabia’s Vision 2030, Forsa aims to empower local businesses and actively contribute to the realization of the Kingdom’s ambitious national goals.

By 2030, we aspire to become one of the leading and most influential general trading companies in Saudi Arabia, creating sustainable opportunities and driving economic growth through dependable partnerships

Mission


Forsa General Trading is committed to becoming a trusted partner for businesses across Saudi Arabia. We deliver competitive pricing, reliable products, and tailored solutions to meet the diverse needs of various industries.
Through strategic partnerships and a customer-centric approach, we strive to support the success of local businesses and contribute meaningfully to the Kingdom’s economic development in alignment with Vision 2030.
